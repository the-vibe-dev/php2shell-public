# Requesting additional research material

The full laboratory scripts and additional evidence are retained privately by Charles Vosburgh. Researchers with an established, verifiable research record may request specific material for peer review, defensive validation, or related legitimate research.

Independent researchers are welcome. Institutional affiliation is not required, and reputation alone does not automatically grant access.

## Initial inquiry

Open a **Research access inquiry** issue with:

1. A verifiable research or professional profile.
2. Relevant publications, upstream contributions, advisories, or other research work.
3. The particular mechanism, claim, or experiment you want to examine.
4. The purpose and scope of the review.

Issues are public. Do not post credentials, confidential target information, identity documents, unpublished vulnerabilities, or exploit payloads. Do not post somebody else's personal information. No identity-document upload is requested.

Charles will review the inquiry and arrange private communication where appropriate. Use an existing established private contact instead when the inquiry itself is confidential.

## Decisions and scope

Requests are considered individually. An issue, positive reputation, or organizational affiliation is not an access approval. The owner decides what to share and may limit the exchange to the evidence necessary for the specified review. Permission to inspect material does not automatically authorize redistribution, publication, or testing of third-party systems.

This public repository contains no automatic script-delivery mechanism, private repository token, private evidence URL, or access credential. No service-level or response-time promise is made.
