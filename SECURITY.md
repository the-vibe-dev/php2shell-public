# Security and publication feedback

This edition distributes non-operational educational examples and derived research records. It does not distribute the private exploit implementations.

For ordinary factual corrections, open an issue with the relevant section and evidence reference. For accidental exposure of sensitive content, contact Charles Vosburgh through an existing private channel or begin a minimal public contact request without reproducing the sensitive content.

Do not put credentials, payloads, confidential logs, or unpublished vulnerability details in a public issue. Additional research access is described in [RESEARCH_ACCESS.md](RESEARCH_ACCESS.md).

The public examples are not target scanners, patch certification, or proof that a deployment is safe. Historical results do not replace current vendor guidance. New contributions must preserve the non-operational public boundary.
